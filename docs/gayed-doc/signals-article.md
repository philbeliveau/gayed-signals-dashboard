CONCLUSION

Last week’s Fed rate cut, despite being wholly anticipated by the market, kept the risk-on momentum going. Tech & growth continue to lead the way with no real sense that cyclicals or anything else could pick up meaningful momentum. Small-caps were the one group outside of mega-cap growth that is still gaining momentum and is clearly feeding off the prospect of lower rates and easier monetary conditions ahead.

The seemingly unstoppable gold rally appears to be taking a bit of a pause here, not surprising given how far it’s run in 2025. That’s brought a bit of balance to the lumber/gold ratio, but not enough to signal risk-on conditions based on those two commodities alone. The recent path of equities and the ongoing leadership from the traditional late cycle winners is still signaling a green light for risk assets. The lack of volatility during a period where volatility traditionally starts picking up is supporting the trend.

Overall, while the Fed rate cut wasn’t a sell the news event for equities, it looks like it might have been for bonds. Long-term yields moved higher by about 15 basis points from their mid-week lows and could be indicating that fixed income investors are starting to move on from pricing in the September cut.
